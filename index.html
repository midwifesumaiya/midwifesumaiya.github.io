A little about me

Care that listens, respects and supports.

I am a Registered Midwife in Bangladesh, committed to providing respectful, woman-centered care through every stage of the maternity journey. My professional approach is grounded in dignity, confidentiality, informed choice and clear communication.

I value safe clinical practice and evidence-informed care, alongside patient education that helps women and families feel heard and better informed. I believe good care recognizes each woman's needs, preferences and right to participate in decisions.
