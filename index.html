A little about me

Care that listens, respects and supports.

Good care starts by seeing the person, not only the condition.

I am a Registered Midwife in Bangladesh with a professional interest in respectful maternity care and maternal and newborn wellbeing. I value dignity, confidentiality, informed choice and clear communication throughout the maternity journey.

My approach is guided by compassion, continued learning and the importance of evidence-informed practice. I believe women and families deserve to be listened to, supported with clear information and included in decisions about their care.
