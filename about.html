My background

Women and families deserve to be heard.

Respect is not an extra part of care. It is part of good care.

I am Sumaiya, a Registered Midwife in Bangladesh. My professional interests include respectful maternity care, maternal health education, newborn wellbeing and supportive communication with women and families.

I value care that recognizes dignity, privacy and informed choice. I also believe professional practice requires humility, teamwork, safe boundaries and a commitment to continued learning.

This website is my professional space to share verified credentials, relevant academic work and learning reflections as my career develops.

My values

Principles I aim to uphold.

These values describe the approach I aspire to bring to professional learning and practice.

01

Respect and dignity

Listen without judgement, protect privacy and respect the individual needs and preferences of every woman.

02

Informed choice

Communicate clearly and support women to understand options and participate in decisions.

03

Safety and accountability

Work within professional scope, seek supervision when needed and escalate concerns appropriately.

04

Learning and reflection

Keep building knowledge, reflect on learning and value feedback and collaboration.
